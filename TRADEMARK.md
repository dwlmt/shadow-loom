## 3. Forks and modified versions (AGPLv3 § 7 § a)

AGPLv3 explicitly permits the Owner to require that modified
versions be re-named to distinguish them from the upstream project
(AGPLv3 § 7, paragraph (a)).

If you publish a modified version of Shadow Loom — under AGPLv3 or
under any other licence permitted by AGPLv3 § 7 — you **must**:

1. Choose a **clearly different name** for your fork that does not
   incorporate "Shadow Loom", "ShadowLoom", "shadow-loom", "Shadow
   Looms", "Shadow-Loom-*", or any name confusingly similar to the
   Marks, including translations, transliterations, and obvious
   misspellings.
2. **Replace** the upstream Shadow Loom logo with your own, or
   remove it entirely. The upstream logo may not be used as the
   primary identifier of a fork.
3. **Update** the application title, the MCP server identity
   string, the UI footer, and any user-facing strings that name the
   product, so that end users are not misled into thinking they are
   interacting with the upstream project.
4. Make clear in your README, splash screen, or `--version` output
   that your fork is a **modified version** of Shadow Loom and is
   **not endorsed** by the Owner.

You may continue to credit Shadow Loom factually under § 1 above
("This project is a fork of Shadow Loom by David Rae Wilmot, ©
2026, AGPL-3.0-or-later, https://github.com/dwlmt/shadow-loom").
