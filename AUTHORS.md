## How to be added

1. Submit a pull request following [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
2. Sign off your commits with `git commit -s` (DCO) or accept the
   CLA via the CLA-assistant bot when prompted on your first PR.
3. Add yourself to the **Contributors** list above as part of the
   PR (or ask a maintainer to do it on merge).

For corporate contributions where rights are held by the employer,
please also arrange a signed corporate CLA per
[`CLA.md` § 6](CLA.md#6-how-to-accept-this-cla) so the entity is
recorded explicitly.
